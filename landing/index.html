FAQ

Is our data ever used to train shared models?

No. We deploy isolated environments with no shared models and no outbound training.

What model do you use?

Currently we provide option between flavours of Gemma3 and LLAMA.

Can you deploy inside our VPC or in an air-gapped setup?

Currently we offer only managed service, but we are planning for hosted model soon.

How do you prevent hallucinations?

We combine confidence gates, citation-first responses, refusal paths and 6 layer Hallucination control to block unsupported answers.

What data sources can you connect to?

Databases, files, Time series data, Graph based data, and APIs.

How fast can we start a pilot?

We are opening private beta soon, You will get an announcement for that.
